## Behavior verification

Driven through the real wms-be HTTP API (`main` at `7db8e05`, local Postgres and Valkey, `OUTBOX_RELAY_POLL_MS=2000`), with tokens from the real `sign-in`. The fresh tenant had one warehouse, BRAND-A (with GST details) and BRAND-B, and SKUs A-TEE, A-CAP and B-SER.

- **As expected:**
  - Client admin, tax details, client-attributed catalog import.
  - Rate card: backdating refused 400 `rate-card-effective-date`; activates from today.
  - Inbound: an operator ASN and a portal ASN for A. A portal ASN naming B's SKU → 404. GRNs against both ASNs. Putaways. Both ASNs reached `received`.
  - **Cross-client wave:** one picklist mixing A and B lines. Picked, packed and dispatched both orders.
  - A's usage: inbound 2 lines 1200 + pick 2 700 + outbound 1 order 2500 = 4400 paise. Storage unbilled until snapshotted.
  - **Portal A:** stock A-only (13/17); orders, ASNs and invoices A-only. B's ids → 404. Operator token on the portal → 403 `role-denied`; portal token on operator routes → 403.
  - **Service report:** the operator and portal bodies are identical apart from `asOf`, over three windows. `ordersDispatched` 1 = the metered outbound order count.
  - **Suspension (set by SQL, see X2):** all six portal reads, the portal ASN POST and sign-in → 403 `client-suspended`. B unaffected.
- **Discrepant:** R1 (a new warehouse needs a restart before taking orders); R2 (createdAt format).
- **Not exercised:** invoice prepare and issue and the dispute drill-down. October has not ended (409 `period-not-ended`), and September is `nothing-to-invoice` because a first card cannot be backdated. These paths rest on `client-invoices.spec.ts` and `invoice-records.spec.ts` only. The web UI click paths were not exercised.

Scripts and logs: `scratchpad/retro21/drive.ts`, `phase2.ts`, `cmp.ts`, `suspend.ts`, `run*.log`. The test tenant remains in the local DB, with BRAND-A restored to `active`.
