- **Queueing:** two e-way bills were queued (log: "value 6160000 paise > national 5000000"); Z logged "needs no e-way bill". Both bills showed only the fixable `transport-incomplete` blocker.
- **Part B:** a transporter ID alone was accepted as Part A. A Road vehicle `mh 12 ab 1234` was normalised to `MH12AB1234`.
- **Export:**
  - version `1.0.0621`, 2 bills, `docDate` `05/10/2026` (IST);
  - `fromStateCode`/`actualFromStateCode` 27/27 and `to*` 29/29; `toGstin` `URP` for B2C;
  - Part-A bill: `transMode` 1, `vehicleNo` `""`, `vehicleType` `R`;
  - pincodes are numbers and `hsnCode` is a string;
  - `totalValue + taxes + OthValue = totInvValue = payable` for both bills.
- **Record and Generate:**
  - recording `341000123456` gave `generated`/`manual`;

  - re-exporting a generated bill gave 409 with `reasons: ["not-pending"]`.
- **HSN summary:** for 2026-10 it shows B2B `0910/NOS/5%`, and B2C `0405/NOS/12%` and `0910/NOS/5%`. Totals reconcile to the three invoices: taxable ₹1,28,800, GST ₹10,290.
