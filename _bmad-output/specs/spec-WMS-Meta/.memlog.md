- (capability) CAP-2 Inventory backbone — ledger, ATP, traceability (FR-4..6): every stock movement an immutable ledger event, on-hand/ATP derived, atomic reservations, batch FEFO + serial traceability. Success: two concurrent reservations for last unit — exactly one wins, deterministic loser outcome; any bin re-computable by replay; dispatched serial full history in one query.
- (capability) CAP-3 Inbound — POs, receiving, QC (FR-7..9): PO lifecycle with open quantities; scan-based GRN ≤4 scans + 1 confirm; partial + blind receipts (blind flagged); QC holds excluded from ATP. Success: partial GRN leaves PO line open with correct remaining qty; over-receipt requires Ops Manager approval; pick request against QC-held stock rejected.
- (capability) CAP-4 Putaway & location management (FR-10,11): directed putaway with suggestion-vs-actual recorded; bin block/merge/retire rules. Success: full/blocked bin rejects placement naming reason; blocking removes bin from suggestions+picking immediately; retire-with-stock rejected naming blockers.
- (capability) CAP-5 Outbound — orders, waves, scan-verified picking (FR-12..15): manual + idempotent channel order ingestion; wave/picklist generation with directed path; offline scan picking with queued replay; short-pick re-planning. Success: duplicate channel payload → exactly one order; offline scans land exactly once in order (idempotency test); batch picklist path visits each bin ≤ once.
- (capability) CAP-6 Packing & shipping (FR-16,17): pack-station verification against picklist; carrier rating, labels, manifests; dispatch closes reservation + writes ledger atomically; tracking writeback to channel. Success: pack scan mismatching picklist rejected with discrepancy named; label-gen failure surfaces retryable error and does not mark dispatched.
- (capability) CAP-7 Transfers & adjustments (FR-18,19): bin/warehouse transfers with in-transit state excluded from both ends' ATP; adjustments with reason codes + threshold approval. Success: over-threshold unapproved adjustment cannot change ATP (sits pending, approver notified); warehouse-transfer legs write correlated events.
- (capability) CAP-8 Cycle counting & audit (FR-20,21): ABC-scheduled + on-demand counts without ops freeze; variance records route to approval with ledger history attached. Success: concurrent picks during count flagged not lost; variance resolution audit-logged; recount replaces expected basis.
- (capability) CAP-9 Replenishment & reorder intelligence (FR-22,23): per-SKU/Warehouse reorder points, alerts ≤5min from breach, suggested POs (never auto-submitted), expiry/aging alerts for batch-tracked SKUs. Success: drafted PO editable before submission; batch within expiry lead days appears on expiry dashboard and in FEFO picks.
- (capability) CAP-10 Channel integrations & oversell protection (FR-24,25): Shopify/Amazon.in/Flipkart order ingestion + near-real-time availability sync with per-channel Safety Buffers + backorder policy; fulfillment writeback. - (capability) CAP-11 Compliance — GST & e-way bills (FR-26): GST-compliant invoices per dispatch from SKU GST/HSN + place-of-supply; single + batch e-way generation; failure retryable, never blocks dispatch. - (capability) CAP-12 Dashboard, notifications & audit trail (FR-27,28): real-time KPIs per warehouse reconciling to ledger as projections; global audit trail exportable.
